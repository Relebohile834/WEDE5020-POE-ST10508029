Keep Hope Alive. Drive Social Change.

Hope SA Foundation works to create positive change in marginalized communities through food aid, disaster relief, healthcare, education, water solutions, poverty alleviation and gender-based violence prevention.

Donate Now Explore Our Works

What We Do

Food Aid

Food drives, hot meals and meals and food hampers support families facing food insecurity.

Disaster Relief

Emergency support responds to communities affected by disasters and crises.

Healthcare

Healthcare initiatives aim to support communities with access to essential care.

Education

Education and skills development form part of Hope SA's longer-term empowerment approach.

Safer Women Drive

The initiative raises awareness around gender-based violence and distributes safety and care packs.

Community Empowerment

Planned skills training and community garden initiatives aim to build opportunity and resilience.

You Can Make a Difference

Support Hope SA's work by donating, volunteering, becoming a partner or sharing its initiatives with your community.
