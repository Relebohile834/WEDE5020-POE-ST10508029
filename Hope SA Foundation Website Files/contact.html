Areas of Community Outreach

Hope SA's published project information identifies community distribution and outreach in and around Johannesburg, Pietermaritzburg and Durban/KwaZulu-Natal. These should be treated as areas of operation rather than automatically as formal office locations.

Johannesburg & Gauteng

Published outreach areas include Diepsloot, Alberton, Tembisa, Brixton, Fietas, Rondebult, Actonville, Coronationville and Windmill Park.

Pietermaritzburg

Hope SA identifies Pietermaritzburg and surrounding communities as part of its disaster-relief and food-support distribution network

Durban and KwaZulu-Natal

Published distribution areas include Reservoir Hills, Phoenix, Verulam, Tongaat, Chatsworth, Shallcross, Merebank, Mobeni and Nanoti
