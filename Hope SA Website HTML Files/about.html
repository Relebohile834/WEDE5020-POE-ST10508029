About Hope SA Foundation

Empowering people, feeding people and giving people hope.

Who We Are

Hope SA Foundation is a South African non-profit organisation focused on supporting marginalised communities. Its work responds to urgent needs such as hunger, disaster impacts, healthcare needs and gender-based violence while also promoting education and empowerment

Our Vision

Hope SA states that its vision is to address poverty, inequality and gender-based violence through strategic initiatives in education, healthcare and disaster relief, while aligning its work with the United Nations

Our Mission

The organisation's mission is to foster hope and positive change in marginalised communities across South Africa and the African continent. Its focus areas include disaster relief, poverty alleviation, water solutions, healthcare, gender-based violence prevention and education.

Our Approach

  • Respond to urgent community needs.
  • Provide practical support such as food and essential supplies.
  • Raise awareness around social issues including gender-based violence.
  • Support education, skills development and community empowerment
  • Work with donors, volunteers and partners to extend community impact.
Organisation information: Hope SA publishes NPO number 250-962 and states that it has PBO status, with Section 18A certificates issued on request.
