Initiatives

Campaigns and responses created to address urgent and ongoing community challenges.

Safer Women Drive

Started in 2020, the #SaferWomen Drive focuses on education and awareness around gender-based violence. Hope SA describes care packs containing items such as sanitary products, safety alarms or pepper spray, toiletries, a book, chocolate and an encouraging note.

Daily Food Programmes & Food Hampers

Monthly food parcels support families in need. The organization requests items such as rice or maize, soup, canned foods, pasta, lentils and hygiene products, as well as financial contributions.

KZN Flood Relief

Hope SA has used its disaster-relief work to support communities in KwaZulu-Natal following severe flooding and related disruption to essential services.

Disaster Relief Appeals

Hope SA publishes urgent appeals when communities experience major disasters or crises, encouraging donations of essential supplies and financial support.

16 Days of Activism

Hope SA has participated in awareness activities connected with the annual 16 Days of Activism campaign against gender-based violence.

International Emergency Appeals

The initiatives section also contains emergency appeals responding to international disasters, such as the 2023 Turkey earthquake response.

Content note: Initiative pages will be updated whenever we launch or close a campaign.
